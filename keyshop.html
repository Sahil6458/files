Ask Us Anything
Do you offer wholesale pricing?
Yes, we offer highly competitive wholesale pricing for bulk orders. The more you order, the better the rate. Contact us directly for a custom quote tailored to your business needs.
What types of car keys do you supply?
We supply transponder keys, smart/proximity keys, flip/remote keys, and remote key shells for all major car brands including Toyota, Honda, Ford, BMW, Hyundai, Maruti Suzuki and more.
Do you sell key maker machines for beginners?
Absolutely. We carry machines suited for beginners entering the locksmith trade as well as advanced professional setups. Our team can help you choose the right machine for your skill level and budget.
What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?
MOQ varies by product category. Key blanks and shells have a low MOQ while machines can be ordered individually. Contact us for specific MOQ details on any product.
Do you provide after-sales support?
Yes, absolutely. Our team provides full after-sales technical support, especially for key machines and programmer devices. We offer onboarding guidance, troubleshooting, and ongoing assistance.
How fast do you ship orders?
We process confirmed orders within 24–48 hours. Delivery times depend on location and order size. We work with reliable logistics partners to ensure your stock arrives quickly and in perfect condition.
